# Editorial calibration

Use this when establishing the editorial standard or resolving an ambiguous intervention. These principles explain judgments; they do not prescribe the shape of every piece. This public reference preserves the reusable lessons from private editorial research without reproducing the manuscript, comments, editor identity, or private source locations. No private document is required to use the skill.

## Source hierarchy and limits

1. **Current author instruction:** preserve the original message, flow, voice, and words; suggestions should fundamentally improve clarity and avoid introducing AI slop. Default output: prioritized critique with targeted before/after edits and reasons. The current brief controls when older examples conflict.
2. **Observed editorial practice:** private manuscript research informed the principles below. An editor's proposal is evidence of a possible intervention, not proof that the author accepted it or that the new wording is factually correct.
3. **[Compound-writing baseline](https://github.com/amanaiproduct/amans-skills/blob/main/plugins/compound-writing/skills/compound-writing/SKILL.md):** its voice analysis and critique workflow supply useful principles, but generation-oriented rules and cadence prescriptions should not be imported wholesale into the evaluation of human prose.
4. **Published samples linked from Amank.ai:** useful evidence of range and voice, not a flawless quality target or evidence of an editor's preferences.

When inspecting tracked changes, distinguish pending proposals from accepted text. Reconstructing a version before pending suggestions cannot establish wording before unavailable historical edits. A flattened export may combine mutually exclusive alternatives; establish a coherent review input before diagnosing writing problems.

## Editorial moves worth considering

### Supply context at the point of need

When a person, organization, or example first appears, provide the context the intended reader needs to understand why it matters. An expert audience may already have that context. Do not routinely add credentials, and do not use a current job title to establish a historical one.

### Reduce syntactic work while retaining the thought

Make the subject and behavior legible when tangled syntax hides them. Preserve the surrounding story; a clearer sentence is not a reason to replace the whole introduction.

Choose direct verbs and remove redundant words when they contribute nothing. A conversational qualifier may express a real correction, uncertainty, or emphasis. Its presence alone does not justify deletion.

### Bridge a missing logical step

Identify the relationship the reader needs: what limitation motivates the next topic, or how a mechanism affects an outcome. Merely announcing the next section does not supply that relationship. Prefer a bridge grounded in the author's existing reasoning; ask for the missing reasoning if it is absent.

### Make headings and exercises useful to a reader

A heading should help the reader anticipate the section's role. An exercise should make clear what the reader is choosing or inspecting, which dimensions matter, and what output is expected.

The aim is orientation and executable instruction. A conversational question heading may already work well. Do not replace every heading with a formal taxonomy or add exercises to genres that do not call for them.

### Tighten the scope of a definition or observation

Help the reader identify who acts, what they inspect or change, and which criteria they apply. Preserve exactly what the evidence supports. A cleaner statement is not permission to infer an unstated sample size, metric, or causal relationship.

## Calibration is not imitation

A proposed edit may clarify a thesis while also strengthening its certainty beyond the evidence. Identify the useful function, then preserve or query claim strength. Formal register and punctuation in prior editorial work do not override the author's current brief.

Watch for semantic changes disguised as cleanup:

- Turning one person's experience into a claim about a whole profession broadens the source.
- Replacing an exact scope or measurement with a vague intensifier loses precision.
- Removing a qualification around example criteria can accidentally prescribe those criteria for every product.
- Smoothing the grammar of an analogy can switch who performs the action and change its meaning.

These are reasons to preserve or question the wording, not reasons to make a broader rewrite. Brevity alone does not justify losing provenance or precision.

Editorial proposals can also introduce mechanical errors or contain requests that are not finished prose. Keep instructions separate from author text, reconcile overlapping alternatives, and reread replacements with their neighbors. Counts of revision markup do not necessarily count independent editorial decisions.

## Voice range from published writing

- [Every PM Should Be Building Skills](https://amankhan1.substack.com/p/every-pm-should-be-building-skills): skepticism followed by firsthand use and concrete explanation. Preserve candor; do not manufacture a conversion story.
- [Building AI Product Sense with a Personal OS](https://amankhan1.substack.com/p/building-ai-product-sense-with-a): workflows, prompts, and visible artifacts explain the mechanism. Preserve useful specificity while distinguishing demonstrations from broader causal claims.
- [Beyond Vibe Checks](https://amankhan1.substack.com/p/beyond-vibe-checks-a-pms-complete): a sustained example explains evaluation and weak proxies. Apply the same lesson to editing: a surface feature is not the reader outcome. Exclude its third-person guest introduction from Aman voice evidence.
- [How AI Prototyping Tools Actually Work](https://amankhan1.substack.com/p/how-ai-prototyping-tools-actually): curiosity about how the system works. Preserve the artifact and mechanism; separate observed behavior from inferred history.
- [I'm Writing an O'Reilly Book](https://amankhan1.substack.com/p/im-writing-an-oreilly-book): economical, warm personal communication; explicitly distinguishes deeper book walkthroughs from posts.

These samples suggest candid practitioner knowledge, plain explanation, and attention to what a reader can do. They do not justify mandatory anecdotes, vulnerability, familiar transitions, bolding, numbered frameworks, or short sentences. Exclude guest authors, embedded quotations, promotional boilerplate, and code when inferring prose preferences. Published writing can itself benefit from editing.
